Software - CachyOS
CachyOS gave me the possibility to use arch without having to worry about GPU driver configuration. Ricing with modules from different builds and learning to make my own as well.


Software - Samsung DEX
Galaxy S devices have the ability to simulate a desktop environment with Android when connecting to an external display. S Tablets can enter DEX mode on their own, paired with a mouse and keyboard it becomes a compact Android laptop.


Hardware - Womier WK61
A decent budget mechanical keyboard in the 60% format that is very reliable. It's got north facing LEDs and OUTEMU Red linear switches on hotswap sockets.
